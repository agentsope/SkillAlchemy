This is an authored C01–C04 application contract. Exact defaults are package choices, not evidence of a best duration, format or style. The applications below use invented demo materials, not transplanted creator cases.

Input: “做一个 15 秒的 AI 产品发布短片，科技感、节奏快、竖屏。”

Preserve 15 seconds, vertical, technology mood and fast rhythm. Product name, actual features, logo, proof and release date remain unknown.
